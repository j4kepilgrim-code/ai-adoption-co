Offer 01

Training Programme

For teams who have the tools and need to learn how to use them. A structured programme that takes your team from Level 0 to Level 2 — and identifies who goes further.

Format
Half-day on-site
Team size
10–100 people
Outcome
Level 0 → Level 2 in a day
Follow-up
30-day pulse check
What you get
01
Pre-training baseline
We measure where your team is before we start. Gated registration means 100% response rate. Sentiment data plus observed capability during the session.
02
Training Day
Half-day on-site. Labs, hands-on exercises, real examples pulled from your business. Not a lecture. People build things during the session.
03
Data safety briefing
Tailored to the specific AI tools your team is using. What's safe to share, what isn't, and why. Plain English, no legal theatre.
04
The Chain
Goal → Model → Context → Reasoning → Tool → Hand-off. A framework your team will use to reason through any AI workflow, now and in the future.
05
30-day pulse check
A 15-minute conversation with the sponsor or champion. Real usage data, observable artefacts, honest read on what's stuck.
06
Written capability report
The delta. How many people moved from which level to which. What's been built. What to do next. Board-presentable.
What makes it different
Model agnostic
Works for Claude, ChatGPT, or any mix. We're not here to steer you toward a particular tool — we work with whatever you've already bought.
Hands-on, not lectures
Labs and real examples from your business, not a slide deck. People build something during the session. The session ends with artefacts, not just notes.
Measurable outcomes
We baseline before, measure after, give you the delta. "At baseline, 3 of 18 people had set up a Project. At 30 days, 16 of 18 had." That's the kind of evidence you get — observable capability, not self-rated comfort.
Teach to fish
Your team learns the method — the Chain — not just a set of prompts to copy. The goal is that they can reason through the next workflow themselves, without us in the room.
Who it's best for
Teams that have just bought enterprise AI plans and don't know where to start. Or teams where initial excitement has stalled — people opened it once, drafted an email, closed the tab — and the CEO is starting to ask where the ROI is. This is the reset.
