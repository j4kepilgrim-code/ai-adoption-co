The Engagement

License → Outcome

You bought the licences. Your team has logins. Not much else is happening. This is a three-week engagement to change that — permanently, without depending on us.

How it works
Week 1
Diagnose
+

Two parallel tracks run simultaneously across week one.

  • Sentiment survey — whole org, 10 min, self-serve. Captures comfort, actual usage, what's working, what's feared.
  • Process interviews — 6–10 people, 30 min each, across functions. Finds where time actually goes and which workflows are ready.
Week 2
Synthesise
+

Raw data becomes two artefacts you can act on immediately.

  • Literacy heatmap — your org by team and seniority, showing who's where on the adoption curve.
  • Ranked use-case backlog — 8–12 workflows scored on frequency, repeatability, judgment required, and stickiness. Top 1–2 become workshop targets.
Week 3
Workshop
+

Half-day, on-site or remote. Five parts built around the chain:

  • Foundation (20 min) — mental model, mirror the survey data, introduce the chain.
  • Walk the chain (30 min) — one worked example, whole room, live.
  • Spot & pick (20 min) — argue the backlog, form breakout groups around workflows that matter to them.
  • Build in groups (60 min) — 2–3 people per workflow. Fill in the chain. We rotate and coach.
  • Share-back + hand-off (50 min) — each group demos, patterns surfaced, owners named, 30-day check-ins set.
