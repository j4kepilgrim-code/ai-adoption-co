What you get
01
Workflow design sessions
We scope what's being built and why before writing a single prompt. What does success look like? Who owns it? What data does it need? What happens when it breaks?
02
End-to-end build
Prompts, persistent workspaces, integrations, documentation. Built to work reliably, not just impressively in a demo.
03
Embedded in your tools
Not a separate AI tab your team has to remember to open. Built into the tools and processes where work already happens.
04
Documentation & ownership
Every workflow gets a named human owner, a one-page SOP, and a review cadence. Things don't break silently — there's someone watching.
05
Ongoing maintenance
AI outputs drift as models update, data changes, and business processes evolve. We keep the workflow working — and tell you when the scope has changed enough to warrant a rebuild.
Worth saying plainly
"We tell you when to stop paying us. Some workflows reach a point where they're stable and the management contract is no longer needed. We'll be honest about that."
What makes it different
We don't build things you should build yourselves
Offer 1 exists to build your team's capability. We only take on Build & Manage for workflows that genuinely shouldn't sit on your team's plate — because they're too technical, too specialist, or operationally too critical to leave to someone learning on the job.
Honest about what "manage" means
Not vague ongoing support. A specific review cadence, observable quality metrics, and a clear definition of what we're maintaining and why. When the workflow is stable and the management contract is no longer earning its cost, we'll say so.
Scoped, not open-ended
We build specific, defined workflows — not an open-ended managed AI service. Everything starts with a clear scope document. You know what you're buying before we start.
Who it's best for
Organisations with specific, complex workflows that need AI but don't want the burden of building or maintaining them internally. Usually comes after an Assessment — the Assessment surfaces the workflows, and Build & Manage handles the ones your team shouldn't own. Not right as a first engagement unless the workflow is clearly defined and the internal capability question has been answered.
