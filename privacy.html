Privacy Policy

How Capable AI handles your information.

Last updated
16 May 2026

Capable AI ("we", "us", "our") takes your privacy seriously. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how long we keep it, and your rights.

We comply with the Australian Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

What We Collect

Through the website

When you contact us through our website, we collect your name, your email address, and any message or details you choose to share.

We also use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our site. This collects anonymised information such as pages visited, time on site, approximate location based on IP address, and device type. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking using the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Through Assessment engagements

When we run an AI Opportunity Assessment for a client organisation, we collect survey responses from employees, interview notes from conversations with heads of department, and any documents the client chooses to share with us.

This information is collected only with the explicit agreement of the client organisation, and we are clear with employees about how the data will be used before they participate.

How We Use It

Information collected through the website is used to respond to enquiries, send information you've requested, and improve our website and services.

Information collected during Assessment engagements is used only to produce the diagnostic findings and presentation for that client. It is not used for any other purpose, shared with third parties, or aggregated with data from other clients.

How Long We Keep It

Contact information from website enquiries is kept for as long as needed to respond to your enquiry. If we begin a working relationship, we keep your details for the duration of that engagement plus 24 months.

Assessment engagement data, including surveys, interviews, and notes, is kept for 12 months after the engagement ends, after which it is deleted. If you would like your data deleted sooner, you can contact us at any time.

Anonymised analytics data is kept for 14 months by Google Analytics, after which it is automatically deleted.

Who We Share It With

We do not sell your information.

We do not share your information with third parties, except where required to deliver our services. Email correspondence is handled through standard email providers such as Google. Website analytics are provided by Google Analytics. We may use AI tools such as Claude and ChatGPT to assist with our own work, but we do not put identifiable client data into consumer-tier AI plans.

If we ever need to share your data with a third party for a specific reason, for example if you ask us to, we will tell you first.

Your Rights

Under the Australian Privacy Act, you have the right to ask what information we hold about you, ask us to correct information that is wrong or out of date, ask us to delete your information, and make a complaint about how we have handled your information.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at j4kepilgrim@gmail.com.

If you are not satisfied with how we have handled your complaint, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information we hold. Data is stored on secure cloud services, accessed through password-protected accounts, and shared only with people who need it to do the work.

We use AI tools that have business-grade data agreements in place. We do not use consumer-tier AI plans for any work that involves identifiable client information.

Changes To This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The most recent version will always be on this page, with the date last updated at the top.

Contact

Questions about privacy or this policy: j4kepilgrim@gmail.com

Capable AI is based in Sydney, Australia.
