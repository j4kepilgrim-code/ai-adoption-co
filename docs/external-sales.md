## Offer 1 — Training Programme

**For teams who have the tools and need to learn how to use them.**

A structured programme that takes your team from Level 0 (has the login, doesn't know what to do with it) to Level 2 (building repeatable workflows with documents, examples, and persistent workspaces). Some of your people will go further — to Level 3, 4, or 5.

What you get:

- A pre-training baseline so we know where your team is starting
- A half-day on-site Training Day — labs, hands-on, on real examples from your business
- Data safety and privacy briefing tailored to which AI tools you're using
- The Chain — a framework your team will use forever to reason through any AI workflow
- 30-day pulse check to measure the uplift
- A written report showing the capability shift, what your team has built, and what to do next

What makes this different:

- Model agnostic — works for Claude, ChatGPT, or any mix
- Hands-on, not lectures — labs, real examples, your team building things during the session
- Measurable outcomes — we baseline before, measure after, give you the delta
- Teach to fish — your team learns the method, doesn't depend on us afterwards

Best for: teams that have just bought enterprise AI plans, or teams where usage has stalled and you need to reset.

## Offer 2 — AI Maturity & Opportunity Assessment

**For organisations who need to know where they stand and where to focus.**

A bespoke assessment of your team's current AI maturity and the workflows in your business where AI would have the highest impact. Built on a combination of organisational survey data, 1:1 interviews with key team members, and observed usage patterns.

What you get:

- Whole-organisation sentiment and capability survey
- 6–10 thirty-minute interviews with leaders and team members across functions
- Literacy heatmap showing where your people sit on the AI maturity scale, by team and seniority
- Ranked opportunity backlog — 8–12 candidate workflows scored on impact, effort, repeatability, and stickiness
- Executive summary with recommendations on where to start and what to outsource
- Board-ready presentation of the findings

What makes this different:

- Not a generic AI strategy deck — built specifically from your team's data
- Independent — we don't sell software, so the recommendations aren't biased toward any tool
- Actionable — every recommendation comes with a clear next step

Best for: organisations beyond the basics who need a map of where AI fits in their operations, or boards and leadership teams who need a credible read on AI readiness before committing to a strategy.

## Offer 3 — Build & Manage

**For organisations who want specific AI workflows built and maintained for them.**

We design, build, and maintain AI workflows for parts of your business where building internal capability doesn't make sense — because the work is too technical, too specialist, or too operationally critical to leave to someone learning on the job.

What you get:

- Workflow design sessions to scope what's being built and why
- End-to-end build — prompts, persistent workspaces, integrations, documentation
- Embedded into your team's actual tools, not a separate AI tab
- Documentation, owner assignment, and review cadence so it doesn't break silently
- Ongoing maintenance and optimisation as your business evolves

What makes this different:

- We don't build things you should build yourselves. We help you build internal capability through Offer 1, and only build the things that genuinely shouldn't sit on your team's plate.
- We tell you when to stop paying us. Some workflows reach a point where they're stable and the management contract is no longer needed. We'll be honest about that.

Best for: organisations with specific, complex workflows that need AI but don't want the burden of building or maintaining them internally.

## How they fit together

Most clients walk a natural path through the three offers.

**Start with Training.** Your team builds capability. Your champions emerge — the people who lean in, ask better questions, help their colleagues. We measure the uplift at 30 days.

**Move to Assessment when you're ready.** If your champions are starting to push but the wider organisation isn't keeping up, the Assessment gives you the map. It tells you where the real opportunities are and what to do about them.

**Add Build & Manage selectively.** Some workflows from the Assessment will be best owned by your team. Others — typically the more technical or operationally critical ones — are better outsourced. We'll be straight with you about which is which.

You don't have to take all three. Many clients only need Training. Some go straight to Assessment if they already have AI maturity but need direction. Some come to us for a specific Build project. The path depends on where you are.
