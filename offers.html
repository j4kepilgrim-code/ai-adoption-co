How they fit together

Most clients walk a natural path through the three offers. You don't have to take all three — many only need Training. But here's how the journey usually looks.

01
Start with Training
Your team builds capability. Your champions emerge — the people who lean in, ask better questions, help their colleagues. We measure the uplift at 30 days and tell you honestly where you are.
02
Move to Assessment when you're ready
If your champions are pushing forward but the wider organisation isn't keeping up, the Assessment gives you the map. It tells you where the real opportunities are and what to do about them. We only recommend this once we've seen appetite inside the organisation.
03
Add Build & Manage selectively
Some workflows from the Assessment will be best owned by your team. Others — typically the more technical or operationally critical ones — are better outsourced. We'll be straight with you about which is which. We don't build things your team should build themselves.
