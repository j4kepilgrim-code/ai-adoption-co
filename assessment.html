What you get
01
Whole-organisation survey
Sent to every person in the company. Captures comfort, actual usage, what's working, and what's feared. Paired with admin console data where available.
02
6–10 in-depth interviews
Thirty minutes each, across functions and seniority levels. We find out where time actually goes and which workflows are genuinely ripe for AI.
03
Literacy heatmap
Your organisation mapped by team and seniority, showing who sits where on the AI maturity scale. Surfaces the bimodal pattern most orgs don't see.
04
Ranked opportunity backlog
8–12 candidate workflows, scored on frequency, repeatability, judgment required, and stickiness. Prioritised with clear rationale.
05
Executive summary
Where to start, what to outsource, and what to build in-house. Every recommendation with a clear next step, not just an observation.
06
Board-ready presentation
A structured presentation of the findings, built to be shared upwards. Evidence-based, plain English, no jargon.
What makes it different
Not a generic AI strategy deck
Everything in the output is built from your team's data — survey responses, interview notes, observed usage patterns. There are no templated findings or copy-pasted frameworks. The heatmap shows your org, the backlog shows your workflows.
Independent
We don't sell software, so the recommendations aren't biased toward any tool. Claude, ChatGPT, or both — it doesn't matter. Our interest is in telling you what's actually true about your situation.
Actionable
Every recommendation comes with a clear next step. Not "explore opportunities in document processing" — but "these three workflows, in this order, starting with this one because of this." The output is a plan, not a report.
Who it's best for
Organisations that have moved past the basics — some people are using AI, some aren't — and need a credible map of where AI actually fits in their operations before committing to a strategy. Also right for boards and leadership teams who need evidence of AI readiness, not just reassurance. We typically recommend this after a Training Day, once we've seen where the champions are.
